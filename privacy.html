Privacy

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, ads, or tracking, and it doesn't collect anything from you directly. Signing up for events happens on Posh or Luma, and chat happens on Discord.

Who we are

Algos & Co. ("we", "us") is a builder collective for tech professionals in the DC, Maryland, and Virginia area. This policy covers algosandco.com (the "site").

What the site collects

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-up forms, no accounts, no comment boxes, and no analytics or advertising tools. We don't sell, rent, or share personal information, and we never have.

Cookies and similar technology

The site doesn't set cookies and doesn't use local storage, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting. That's why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. If we ever add analytics or anything that uses cookies, we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub automatically records basic technical details when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of the visit, to keep the service running and secure. We can't see or access these logs. GitHub's handling of this data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

All fonts and files are served from algosandco.com itself, so loading the site doesn't contact any other company.

Event sign-ups (Posh and Luma)

When you RSVP or buy a ticket, you do it on Posh or Luma, and their privacy policies apply. As the event organizer, we receive the registration details you give them, such as your name, email address, and any answers to sign-up questions. We use these only to run the event: check-in, venue capacity, and updates about that event or future Algos & Co. events. You can unsubscribe from our event emails at any time using the platform's unsubscribe link.

  • Posh privacy policy
  • Luma privacy policy

Discord

Our community lives on Discord. Anything you post there is visible to other members and is governed by Discord's privacy policy. Server moderators can see public member information as Discord allows.

Links to other sites

The site links to Discord, Posh, Luma, and other websites. We're not responsible for how those sites handle your information, so please review their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), state privacy laws in Virginia, Maryland, and elsewhere, and the EU/UK GDPR may give you the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. The only personal information we hold is event registration details received from Posh or Luma. To make a request, email contactalgosandco@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: the site doesn't track you, so these signals are respected by default.

Children

The site and our events are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has shared information with us, email contactalgosandco@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Security

The site is a static website served only over HTTPS. It runs no server code and has no database, so there is no stored visitor data to breach.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the Discord.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email contactalgosandco@gmail.com, or reach us in the Algos & Co. Discord.
